The helper knows every lesson and all the course material. It re-explains a concept another way, works through the maths with you, and answers the questions you would otherwise have to ask a forum.

You'll be talking to an AI, not a person. It explains how the software works; it doesn't give investment advice or tell you how much to invest. Coming with the platform launch.

Example
My position moved 1,200 USDC into margin. Did I lose money?
No. ETH rose, so the short lost value while your spot ETH gained the same amount. Selling 1,200 USDC of that spot gain and moving it into the short's margin keeps it far from liquidation. Your total is unchanged, apart from funding and fees.
Should I raise leverage to earn more?
I can't advise you on that. What I can explain: higher leverage puts more of your capital into the trade, but leaves the short with less margin, so a sharp ETH rally gets it closer to liquidation. Lesson 06 covers the trade-off.
